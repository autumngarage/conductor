## What shipped

- OpenRouter requests that carry Conductor's `max_tokens` ceiling now also send
  the same value as `max_completion_tokens`, matching OpenRouter's current
  completion-budget parameter.
- OpenRouter 402 affordability retries now lower both token-limit fields
  together, so retry payloads cannot keep a stale higher completion ceiling.
- Implicit OpenRouter `call` and tool-loop `exec` requests cap affordability
  retries at the effort-derived budget Conductor originally selected.
- Regression coverage pins the shared OpenRouter transport plus Kimi and
  DeepSeek wrapper payloads to include both token-limit fields.
