## What shipped

- OpenRouter-backed requests now send Conductor's output ceiling as both
  `max_tokens` and `max_completion_tokens`, preventing upstream pricing from
  using a larger implicit completion cap.
- OpenRouter 402 affordability retry payloads now lower both token-limit fields
  together.
- Implicit OpenRouter `call` and tool-loop `exec` retries stay capped at the
  effort-derived budget Conductor selected for the request.
- The release workflow passed source validation, bumped the Homebrew tap,
  installed the released formula, and smoked the released binary.
