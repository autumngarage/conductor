> Completed the v0.11 compatibility-window slice of the simplified Conductor
> work surface by warning on legacy auto-routing controls while preserving
> lower-level explicit provider pins.

## What shipped

- `conductor call`, `conductor review`, and `conductor exec` now emit a
  deprecation warning when successful invocations use legacy auto-routing
  controls such as `--auto`, `--tags`, `--prefer`, `--exclude`, auto-route
  `--effort`, or the matching `CONDUCTOR_*` environment variables.
- The warnings are suppressed for `--json` and `--silent-route` so machine
  consumers keep clean output streams.
- Explicit lower-level provider pins with `--with <provider>` remain quiet and
  compatible, preserving the documented escape hatch while default routing
  moves to job verbs.
- Consumer documentation now marks the legacy auto-routing flags as deprecated
  for v0.11 compatibility and points callers at `ask`, `code`, `research`,
  `review`, `text-review`, and `council`.
- Regression tests cover CLI flag warnings, environment-derived warnings,
  JSON suppression, provider-pin silence, and the review/exec command paths.

## Closes / advances

- **Issues:** Closes #501.
- **Plans:** advances the simplified work-surface and flat-rate-first routing
  direction captured in `root-cause-roadmap` and
  `conductor-blindspots`.
- **Doctrine:** reinforces narrow public interfaces and compatibility-at-boundary
  migration by keeping old flags parseable while making their deprecation
  explicit.

None in the current open issue queue. The hard removal of deprecated flags is a
