## What shipped

- Merge review no longer enters auto-fix for `CODEX_REVIEW_BLOCKED` output
  without actionable findings; it treats that as malformed review output and
  can use fallback review.
- Fix-mode now requires and emits structured `FIX_CLASSIFICATION` lines so
  harnesses can distinguish substantive repairs from style-only churn.
- `CODEX_REVIEW_FIXED` output with missing, duplicate, or malformed fix
  classification is rejected before staging or committing.
- Legacy auto-routing controls on `call`, `review`, and `exec` now warn during
  the v0.11 compatibility window while explicit `--with <provider>` pins remain
  quiet.
- Duplicate stale Alchemist OpenRouter 65,536-token reports were reconciled
  against the v0.10.37/current-tree token-cap fix and upstream Alchemist runtime
  telemetry issue.
- The release workflow passed source validation, bumped the Homebrew tap,
  installed the released formula, and smoked the released binary. Local
  `brew update && brew upgrade autumngarage/conductor/conductor` upgraded this
  machine from 0.10.37 to 0.10.38, and `conductor --version` reports 0.10.38.
- Repo-owned generated delegation snippets were refreshed to stamp v0.10.38.
